FAQ

Do desk dividers make open offices quieter?

They can soften the feel of nearby distractions and reduce visual interruption, but they should not be treated as full soundproofing.

Should every desk use the same divider?

Not always. Shared standards help the office look consistent, but focus zones, reception desks, and training rooms may need different materials or heights.
