Public-space walkthrough

Test reception and training dividers from the visitor’s perspective. Sit in the waiting chair, stand at the counter, and walk behind a training row. The right panel should protect details without making the room feel closed, confusing, or unfriendly.

For training rooms, include storage in the decision. Panels that stack neatly, roll safely, or fit into a labeled closet are far more likely to be used again. If storage is awkward, the divider plan may disappear after the first room reset.
